# v0.100.44

Native TUI UX overhaul: Markdown replies, unified-diff approvals with y/n keys and typing-safe guards, status-icon tool cards, a welcome screen, grouped help, a full slash menu, plain-language outcomes, and provider errors that stop promptly with a specific reason.

The design principles, findings and roadmap are in [the UX review](../ux-review.md).

## What changes for you

- **Approvals** appear in a compact panel above the composer, titled
  `Allow edit to notes.md?` or `Run shell command?`. Edits and writes show a
  unified diff with three lines of context, and shell calls show the literal
  command. Press **y** or **Enter** to allow once, **n** to deny and **Esc** to
  stop the turn. **Ctrl+Y** and **Ctrl+D** still work.
  - A plain y or Enter only counts after a short pause with no other key, so
    typing can't approve.
  - On a terminal too small to show the preview, Allow waits until it can.
  - Invisible and right-to-left control characters are shown as `⟨U+202E⟩`.
- **Assistant replies render Markdown**, including headings, emphasis, lists,
  quotes, links and code. Copying a wrapped paragraph gives you one line back.
- **Tool calls** are one row each: `▸ ✓ read_file · hello.txt · 3ms`.
  - **✗** marks a failed or denied call and **⊘** a cancelled one.
  - A call waiting for you says *waiting for approval*.
- **Provider errors** end the turn right away with a specific reason, such as
  rate limit, credentials, model not found or network. Before, a failing
  provider could keep a turn running for minutes. Automatic retries are
  announced as they happen.
- **Starting out:**
  - A new conversation shows the version, the workspace and the first keys
    to know.
  - The empty composer shows a hint.
  - **F1** help is grouped by task.
  - **/** lists every command.
- Your messages have an accent bar. The Agents sidebar only appears once a turn
  delegates, or when you press **Ctrl+T**.
- Turn outcomes read **✗ Failed: reason**, **⊘ Stopped** or **⚠ Interrupted**
  instead of internal status names.
