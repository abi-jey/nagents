# v0.100.45

Copy anything on screen, keep tool headers still when they expand or finish, and edit the draft anywhere with arrow keys.

## Native TUI

- **Copy anything on screen.** Borders, the status rows, the approval panel,
  help and pickers can now be dragged and copied as painted, one line per row
  without trailing padding. Conversation and draft text still copy as logical
  lines with soft wraps joined.
- **Tool headers stay put.** Expanding a call no longer opens a blank row
  above it, so the header you clicked does not move. Finished calls no longer
  gain a check mark that shifted the label: success turns the label green,
  and failed, denied or cancelled calls keep their colour and status word.
- **Edit the draft anywhere.** ←/→ move by character, Ctrl/Alt+←/→ by word,
  Home/End to the line edges (with Ctrl, the draft's), ↑/↓ between lines of a
  multi-line draft. Typing, Shift+Enter, paste, Backspace and Delete act at
  the cursor (Ctrl/Alt deletes a word), and the composer scrolls to keep the
  caret visible.
